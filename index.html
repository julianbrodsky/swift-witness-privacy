Swift Witness

An offline motorbike racing game for iPhone and iPad. Ride fast, notice what you pass, and answer a quiz at the finish line.

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026.

Swift Witness collects no data. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and never connects to the internet.

Your progress (records, credits and the parts on your bike) is stored only on your device. It is included in your own iCloud or computer backups of the device if you use those, and is deleted when you delete the app. To erase your progress without deleting the app, open Settings inside the game and choose Reset progress. Your sound, music and haptics choices are stored the same way.

No data is shared with any third party, because no data leaves your device. The game asks for no permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, motion or notifications.

Credits in the game are pretend. They cannot be bought, sold or cashed out for anything real, and the game has no purchases.

The game is suitable for all ages and treats every player the same way: nothing is collected from anyone, including children.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
